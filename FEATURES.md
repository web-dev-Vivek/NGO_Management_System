# Features

## Authentication & Profiles
- Clerk passwordless/OAuth authentication
- Automatic Clerk-to-MongoDB user synchronization
- Volunteer profiles with bio, skills, and availability
- Coordinator promotion requests

## Campaigns
- Campaign explorer with filters
- Categories: Education, Healthcare, Environment, Disaster Relief
- Location support
- Campaign image uploads with Multer
- Volunteer registration
- Draft → Pending → Active → Completed lifecycle

## Tasks & Hours
- Assign tasks to registered volunteers
- Priorities: Low, Medium, High, Urgent
- Deadlines and estimated hours
- Check-in/check-out
- Automatic logged-hour calculation
- Coordinator verification and adjustment

## Certificates
- PDF certificates generated with PDFKit
- Unique certificate codes
- Public certificate verification
- Certificate download

## Analytics
- Total users
- Active campaigns
- Volunteer hours
- Certificates issued
- Pending reviews
- Role and campaign-category charts
- User status/role management
