# Consultation

## Suggested Discovery Questions
1. How many volunteers does the NGO currently manage?
2. How are campaigns currently created and approved?
3. How are volunteer hours recorded?
4. Who verifies attendance and hours?
5. How are certificates currently generated?
6. What reports does management need?
7. What user roles and approval rules are required?
8. What data must be retained for auditing?

## Expected Outcome
Convert operational requirements into a prioritized product roadmap and implementation plan.
