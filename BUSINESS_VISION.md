# Business Vision

## Problem
NGOs often manage volunteers, campaigns, attendance, tasks, and recognition through disconnected spreadsheets or manual processes.

## Solution
Provide one centralized system for:
1. Recruiting and managing volunteers
2. Organizing campaigns
3. Delegating and tracking work
4. Verifying contribution hours
5. Issuing verifiable certificates
6. Measuring organizational impact

## Success Metrics
- Active volunteers
- Campaign participation
- Completed tasks
- Verified volunteer hours
- Certificates issued
- Campaign completion rate
- Volunteer retention
